Problem Statement

Accidental Exposure of Sensitive Information During Digital File Sharing

In today's digital world, people frequently share documents, screenshots and images without realizing that these files may contain sensitive personal information. Details such as phone numbers, email addresses, identification numbers, home addresses and hidden document metadata can unintentionally be exposed.

Such information, once shared, may lead to privacy breaches, identity theft or misuse of personal data. However, users often lack an easy and accessible way to identify these risks before sharing their files.

The core problem is the absence of a simple, user-friendly solution that detects potentially sensitive information and helps individuals protect their privacy before sharing digital files.
