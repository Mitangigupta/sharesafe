In-Situ Workflow Sequence

The ShareSafe extension intercepts accidental data leakage at the moment of creation, avoiding the need to manually export files or navigate away from active browser tabs:

1

In-Browser Text Selection / Compose Focus

Select text on any webpage or focus an active compose box in Gmail or WhatsApp Web.

2

Context Menu or 1-Click Draft Trigger

Right-click "Scan with ShareSafe" or click "Scan Compose Box" in the extension popup.

3

Checksum & Risk Verification

Dispatched to /api/scan-text to evaluate mathematical checksums (Verhoeff, Luhn) and context risk multipliers in under 15ms.

4

Defensive Masking & In-Place Replacement

Popup badge lights up with risk level, Privacy Assistant guidance, and 1-click option to replace text directly in your active draft.

Client Privacy Guarantees

Zero Surveillance

No keyloggers, passive sniffing, or DOM tracking. Only processes text explicitly selected or commanded by the user.

Scoped Permissions

Restricted strictly to contextMenus, storage, and activeTab. Zero access to browsing history or global URLs.

Ephemeral Caching

Findings reside exclusively in chrome.storage.session, which is automatically purged when the browser closes.
