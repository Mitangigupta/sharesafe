Proposed Solution

ShareSafe Privacy Protection Application

ShareSafe is a web-based privacy protection application that allows users to upload documents and images before sharing them with others.

1
Upload & Scan
Pre-share upload of PDFs, screenshots & images for local audit.
2
Detect & Inform
Identifies sensitive entities & highlights privacy threat levels.
3
Action & Export
Apply Blur, Hide, Remove or Keep and download safer file.

The application scans the uploaded files to identify potentially sensitive information, highlights the detected content and informs users about the associated privacy risks.

It then gives users the option to take appropriate action, such as:

Blur Context Preserved

Blur sensitive information while retaining the surrounding content.

Visual Effect: +91 98765 43210

Hide Masked Surface

Conceal selected information from view.

Visual Effect: ••••••••••

Remove Permanent Burn-in

Permanently redact sensitive information from the file.

Visual Effect: ██████████

Keep User Verified

Retain the information if the user considers it safe to share.

Visual Effect: Safe Data ✓

After making the necessary changes, users can generate and download a safer version of their original file.

ShareSafe aims to make privacy protection a conscious step before every file is shared.
