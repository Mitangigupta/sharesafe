Project Objectives

ShareSafe Project Objectives

01
OBJECTIVE 1

Minimise Accidental Information Exposure

To reduce the possibility of unintended disclosure of personal and confidential information during digital file sharing by addressing privacy risks that users may otherwise overlook.

Targeted Outcome: Pre-Share Leak Prevention
02
OBJECTIVE 2

Promote Privacy Awareness and Informed Decision-Making

To improve users' understanding of digital privacy by helping them recognise the potential consequences of exposing personal information in different sharing situations.

Targeted Outcome: Conscious Privacy Agency
03
OBJECTIVE 3

Encourage Context-Aware and Responsible File Sharing

To establish a more responsible approach to sharing digital information by ensuring that privacy decisions consider the purpose, recipient and intended audience of a file.

Targeted Outcome: Calibrated Sharing Decisions

Methodological Clarity: Objectives vs. Features

Do not confuse objectives with features. Keep these three objectives focused on outcomes rather than technical functionality.
