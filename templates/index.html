Defensive Architecture

Why ShareSafe Is Different

Most basic redaction tools leave dangerous privacy loopholes. ShareSafe mitigates each critical flaw:

STANDOUT NOVELTY

QR Payload Deep-Inspection (X-Ray)

The Hidden QR Blindspot: When users redact an Aadhaar card or bill, they black out the visible text but leave the QR code intact! ShareSafe decodes the QR payload, flags embedded UPI addresses or demographic XML, and defaults it to being redacted.

True PDF Vector Flattening

Stopping Pseudo-Redactions: Simple black boxes merely overlay visual rectangles in PDFs, leaving selectable text underneath for pdftotext. ShareSafe destroys underlying text streams and rasterizes pages at high DPI so text recovery is impossible.

Algorithmic Checksum Validation

No Alert Fatigue: Rather than flagging random 10 or 12 digit strings, ShareSafe executes the Verhoeff algorithm for Indian Aadhaar and the Luhn algorithm for Credit/Debit cards to catch genuine leaks.

Tamper-Evident Redaction Certificate

Cryptographic Proof: Export a verifiable PDF and JSON receipt containing SHA-256 pre/post file hashes certifying all PII and metadata were wiped, without exposing the raw secret values.

Zero-Leak Metadata Stripping

Location & Camera Wiping: Automatically scrubs EXIF tags, GPS coordinates, camera serials, author history, and PDF /Info dictionaries during export.

Manual Redaction Crosshair Tool

Full Precision: Click and drag custom redaction boxes over handwritten signatures, logos, stamps, or faces that automated OCR might miss.
